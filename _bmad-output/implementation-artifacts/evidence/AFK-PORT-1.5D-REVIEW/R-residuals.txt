## R4 — launcher.budget_policy is never consulted by the kit
    occurrences in lib/afk-commands.sh: 0
    dispatch.sh precedence: $AGENT_FLOW_BUDGET_POLICY, then .launcher.budget_policy,
    then the installed default. The kit reads the first and the third only, so a
    host that declares the policy through the config bands its workers on one
    document and its coordinator on another, with no finding either way.
