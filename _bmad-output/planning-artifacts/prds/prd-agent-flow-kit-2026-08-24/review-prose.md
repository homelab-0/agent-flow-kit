# Editorial Prose Review — Agent Flow Kit Portability and Maintenance

This document exists to help product, architecture, and implementation stakeholders agree on a safe, bounded portability increment and verify that delivery satisfies its frozen behavioral and architectural contracts.

The prose intentionally uses formal normative language (`shall`), compact requirement statements, stable machine-code tokens, and architecture-specific terminology. Those choices support precision and should be preserved. The findings below address only passages where sentence shape or reference clarity impedes comprehension. They assume the moves and merges proposed in `review-structure.md`; no fixes target the stale `Open Questions` passage, and the scope-boundary fix belongs in the surviving consolidated `Increment Boundary` section.
